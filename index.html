VOID — Business & Strategy Architecture

1. Problem Statement

Athletes and fitness enthusiasts face rapid bacterial rot and severe odors in enclosed gear bags, boxing gloves, and cleats. Chemical sprays only add wet residue and mask smells with synthetic perfumes, failing to address trapped sweat.

2. Solution

VOID is an all-natural, reusable moisture and odor vacuum insert. Utilizing activated bamboo charcoal and high-capacity indicating silica gel in an athletic spacer mesh sleeve with dust-proof lining, VOID adsorbs volatile organic compounds and dries equipment from the inside out.

3. Target Market

Combat athletes (boxing, MMA, BJJ), soccer/track runners, high-frequency gym commuters, and specialty sneaker collectors who invest heavily in footwear and gear.

4. Market Sizing (TAM, SAM, SOM)

TAM (Global) $1.4B+ Shoe & gear care
SAM $320M Fitness gear accessories
SOM (Year 1-2) $1.8M D2C & Gym partnerships

5. Revenue Model

Direct-to-Consumer e-commerce packs ($19.99 - $34.99), Subscribe & Save quarterly refill plans (15% recurring margin), and B2B wholesale co-branded units supplied directly to martial arts gyms and fitness clubs.

6. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Micro-influencer sampling with combat sports athletes (unboxing and smell tests), local martial arts gym ambassador placement programs, and viral visual science demonstrations (humidity chambers vs sprays) on short-form video platforms.
